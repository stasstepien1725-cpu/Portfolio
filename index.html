Featured Projects

Live client work

Campaign · Community · Awareness

Nottingham Safeguarding Adults Board

Developed a one-week public awareness campaign for the Nottingham Safeguarding Adults Board as part of National Safeguarding Adults Week. The campaign focused on increasing awareness and improving access to safeguarding support, with particular attention given to underrepresented South Asian communities. Research into community spaces including Punjabi Forest directly informed the targeting strategy. Presented to the Board at the close of the project.

My Role

  • Contributed to campaign planning and idea development
  • Researched target audience and underrepresented communities including Punjabi Forest
  • Developed campaign messaging and tone of voice
  • Refined and improved presentation slides
  • Delivered final presentation to the Nottingham Safeguarding Adults Board

Strategy · Brand · Positioning

British Nordic Walking

Developed a strategic marketing approach for British Nordic Walking with emphasis on brand positioning and improving engagement across different audience segments. The work involved analysing BNW's current positioning, identifying competitive differences against WALX, and proposing a structured 6-month engagement strategy across community, GP, and university channels.

Key Contributions

  • Developed brand positioning comparison between BNW and WALX
  • Contributed to defining a clear strategic direction
  • Created a structured 6-month campaign timeline
  • Proposed multi-channel engagement strategies across community, GP and university sectors
  • Supported presentation delivery
