About Me

Building real skills through real work

I'm a second-year Marketing student at Nottingham Trent University completing the BUSI22000 Marketing Agency module. Through this module I've worked on two live client campaigns — one for the Nottingham Safeguarding Adults Board and one for British Nordic Walking — gaining hands-on experience in campaign planning, market research, and professional communication.

Alongside my studies I'm part of the University Officer Training Corps (UOTC), which has developed my ability to communicate clearly, work effectively under pressure, and operate reliably within a team. These skills feed directly into my project work throughout the module.

I'm drawn to careers in active, fast-paced environments — particularly within sport, events, and lifestyle sectors. This portfolio captures my professional development across the module and the evidence behind it.

Nottingham Safeguarding Adults Board

Developed a one-week public awareness campaign for the Nottingham Safeguarding Adults Board as part of National Safeguarding Adults Week. Presented to the Board at the close of the project.

My Role

  • Refined and improved presentation slides
